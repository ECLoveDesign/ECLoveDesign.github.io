Financial Training Services

Mandatory Training Subjects

To meet the annual training requirement, it's essential that the training sessions are conducted by institutions accredited and recognized by the Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC), like TABF being one of the acknowledged entities. Adherence to this mandate guarantees that professionals remain up-to-date with the dynamic regulatory landscape, ensuring they possess the requisite knowledge and skills to navigate the changing landscape of international finance.

Training for Core Talents

TABF organizes custom curricula, aiming to cultivate talent across various levels within the banking sector. These programs are meticulously designed to cater to the diverse needs of employees at different organizational tiers within banks.

International training

TABF, leading globalization, aims to be the premier choice for organizations seeking to cultivate top-tier international talent through our comprehensive training programs and partnerships. As dedicated leaders in the field, we excel in preparing individuals and businesses to thrive in an increasingly interconnected world, ensuring they have the skills and expertise necessary to succeed on a global scale.

Other International Training Programs

TABF also focuses on enhancing the practical ability of Taiwanese banks to comply with overseas banking regulations. With a specific emphasis on both local and international laws, participants gain valuable insights into regulatory frameworks essential for global compliance. Courses are conducted in prominent financial centers worldwide, including Mainland China, Hong Kong, New York, Singapore, Japan, Vietnam, Cambodia, and more, ensuring comprehensive coverage and practical training in international regulatory practices.

TABF organizes groups for overseas study tours from various organizations based on industry demand for overseas exchanges.

TABF E-learning Platform: Fin Course Click here to learn more.

TABF provides the Fin Course through video broadcast: open on-line courses for banking practitioners, with customized learning schedules and expert insights on hot issues. We have also launched an app to support mobile learning.

The platform has a variety of online courses for financial professionals and now includes language skills and workplace soft skills, creating a comprehensive digital content library for financial learning. It uses diverse digital learning formats to meet the different needs of financial professionals and provides basic financial courses and insights into financial industry trends for the general public, creating a robust and growing financial learning ecosystem.

The Fin Course platform features a multi-dimensional learning structure that focuses on financial practice as the foundation. It offers content ranging from beginner to advanced levels, spanning multiple fields and professional competencies. This structure is designed to meet the learning needs of financial professionals at each stage of their careers.
