Financial Proficiency Testing

TABF is in charge of issuing banking accreditation to enhance the quality of the financial workforce.
Each of the 180,000 banking professionals in Taiwan has an average of five financial certificates issued by TABF.

A Variety of Exam Formats

In TABF, financial tests encompass a variety of formats. These include traditional written tests, where candidates demonstrate their knowledge through pen-and-paper assessments. Additionally, computer-based tests offer a digital platform for examination, allowing for efficient processing and immediate feedback. Furthermore, Portable Computer Based Testing (PCBT) extends accessibility by enabling candidates to take assessments on portable devices, facilitating flexibility and convenience in test-taking locations.
